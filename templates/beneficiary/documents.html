Document Requirements

Income Certificate

An official document verifying your annual household income issued by a government authority.

Format: PDF, JPG, JPEG, PNG (Max 5MB)

Identity Photo

A recent passport-sized photograph with a white background.

Format: JPG, JPEG, PNG (Max 2MB)

Land Ownership Document

Document proving ownership of the land where construction will take place.

Format: PDF, JPG, JPEG, PNG (Max 5MB)

Bank Account Details

First page of your bank passbook or a cancelled cheque to verify your account details.

Format: PDF, JPG, JPEG, PNG (Max 5MB)

Important Information

All documents must be clear and legible. Blurry or incomplete documents may delay the approval process.
